Title: VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Problem:
Users receive a VPN connection timeout error while trying to connect to the corporate network.

Possible Causes:
- Poor internet connection
- Incorrect VPN credentials
- Firewall blocking VPN traffic

Resolution:
1. Verify internet connection.
2. Restart the VPN client.
3. Verify username and password.
4. Update the VPN client.
5. Contact IT support if the issue persists.
