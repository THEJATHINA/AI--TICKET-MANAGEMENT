Title: Firewall Troubleshooting Guide

Category: Security

Problem:
Applications or VPN connections are blocked by the firewall.

Possible Causes:
- Firewall rules blocking traffic
- Incorrect security policy
- Closed ports

Resolution:
1. Review firewall rules.
2. Allow the application or VPN.
3. Open required ports.
4. Restart the firewall service.
5. Test the connection again.
