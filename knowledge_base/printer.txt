Title: Printer Troubleshooting Guide
Category: Hardware
Problem:
The printer is not printing documents or is showing as offline.
Possible Causes:
- Printer is powered off
- Printer is disconnected from the network
- Paper jam
- Printer driver is missing or outdated
Resolution:
1. Check if the printer is powered on.
2. Verify the USB or network connection.
3. Clear any paper jams.
4. Restart the printer.
5. Reinstall or update the printer driver.
6. Print a test page.
